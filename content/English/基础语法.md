## 一、💡 先记住英语句子的核心

英语句子最常见的结构是：

> 主语 + 动词 + 其他成分

例如：

- I work. —— 我工作。
- She likes coffee. —— 她喜欢咖啡。
- They are students. —— 他们是学生。

英语语法的关键是：动词会随着“时间”和“主语”发生变化。

## 二、🔗 `be` 动词：am、is、are、was、were

`be` 动词通常表示“是、在、处于某种状态”。

### 2.1 一般现在时

| 主语 | `be` 动词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am happy. |
| He / She / It | is | She is a teacher. |
| You / We / They | are | They are busy. |

例句：

- I am a student. —— 我是一名学生。
- He is at home. —— 他在家。
- We are ready. —— 我们准备好了。

### 2.2 否定句

在 `be` 后面加 `not`：

- I am not tired.
- She is not busy.
- They are not at home.

常见缩写：

- is not = isn’t
- are not = aren’t
- am not 通常写作 `I’m not`

### 2.3 `be` 动词疑问句

把 `am / is / are` 放到主语前面：

| 陈述句 | 疑问句 |
|---|---|
| You are ready. | Are you ready? |
| He is a doctor. | Is he a doctor? |
| They are happy. | Are they happy? |

回答：

- Are you tired?
- Yes, I am.
- No, I’m not.

过去时使用：

- I / He / She / It → `was`
- You / We / They → `were`

例句：

- I was tired yesterday.
- They were at school yesterday.
- Was she busy?
- Were you at home?

> [!tip] 记忆方法
> `be` 动词本身就可以构成疑问句，不需要 `do / does / did`。

## 三、🔄 一般现在时

一般现在时主要表示：

- 经常发生的动作
- 日常习惯
- 客观事实
- 长期状态

例如：

- I get up at seven every day.
- The sun rises in the east.
- She works in a hospital.

### 3.1 肯定句

普通主语使用动词原形：

> I / You / We / They + 动词原形

- I like English.
- They play football.
- We live in Beijing.

第三人称单数使用动词 `s` 或 `es`：

> He / She / It + 动词 `s` 或 `es`

- He likes English.
- She works in a bank.
- It rains a lot here.

常见变化：

- work → works
- like → likes
- watch → watches
- go → goes
- study → studies

### 3.2 否定句

使用 `do not` 或 `does not`：

> 主语 + do/does not + 动词原形

- I don’t like coffee.
- They don’t work here.
- She doesn’t like coffee.
- He doesn’t play tennis.

> [!warning] 注意
> `doesn’t` 后面必须使用动词原形。
>
> 错误：She doesn’t likes coffee.
>
> 正确：She doesn’t like coffee.

### 3.3 一般现在时疑问句

使用：

> Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形？

- Do you like English?
- Do they live here?
- Does he work in a bank?
- Does she speak Chinese?

回答：

- Do you like tea?
- Yes, I do.
- No, I don’t.

- Does he play football?
- Yes, he does.
- No, he doesn’t.

## 四、⏪ 一般过去时

一般过去时表示过去已经发生并结束的动作或状态。

常见时间词：

- yesterday —— 昨天
- last week —— 上周
- last year —— 去年
- two days ago —— 两天前
- in 2020 —— 在2020年

例如：

- I watched a movie yesterday.
- She visited her parents last week.
- They went to Shanghai last year.

### 4.1 普通动词的过去式

规则动词通常加 `-ed`：

- work → worked
- play → played
- watch → watched
- visit → visited

不规则动词需要单独记忆：

| 原形 | 过去式 |
|---|---|
| go | went |
| eat | ate |
| see | saw |
| buy | bought |
| have | had |
| do | did |

### 4.2 过去时否定句

使用：

> 主语 + did not + 动词原形

- I didn’t watch TV.
- She didn’t go to school.
- They didn’t buy a car.

> [!warning] 注意
> `didn’t` 后面必须使用动词原形。
>
> 错误：She didn’t went home.
>
> 正确：She didn’t go home.

### 4.3 过去时疑问句

使用：

> Did + 主语 + 动词原形？

- Did you watch the movie?
- Did she go home?
- Did they buy a new car?

回答：

- Did you see John?
- Yes, I did.
- No, I didn’t.

`be` 动词的过去时不使用 `did`：

- Was he tired?
- Were they at home?

## 五、❓ 疑问句的基本形式

### 5.1 一般疑问句

一般疑问句可以用“Yes”或“No”回答。

#### `be` 动词

> Am / Is / Are / Was / Were + 主语？

- Are you busy?
- Is she your sister?
- Was he tired?
- Were they at school?

#### 一般现在时普通动词

> Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形？

- Do you like music?
- Does he play football?

#### 一般过去时普通动词

> Did + 主语 + 动词原形？

- Did you go there?
- Did she call you?

### 5.2 特殊疑问句

常见疑问词：

| 疑问词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| what | 什么 | What do you want? |
| where | 哪里 | Where do you live? |
| when | 什么时候 | When did he arrive? |
| who | 谁 | Who is she? |
| why | 为什么 | Why are you sad? |
| how | 怎样 | How do you go to work? |

基本结构：

> 疑问词 + 一般疑问句？

例如：

- Where do you work?
- What does she like?
- When did they leave?
- Why are you tired?
- How was your weekend?

## 六、⚠️ 最容易犯的错误

| 错误 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| He don’t like tea. | He doesn’t like tea. |
| Does she likes music? | Does she like music? |
| Did you went home? | Did you go home? |
| She are happy. | She is happy. |
| They was tired. | They were tired. |
| Are you like coffee? | Do you like coffee? |

> [!tip] 一句话记忆
> `be` 动词自己提问；普通动词借助 `do / does / did` 提问。

## 七、✍️ 练习题

填入正确形式：

1. I ___ a student.
2. She ___ not busy.
3. They ___ at home yesterday.
4. He ___ English every day.
5. She doesn’t ___ coffee.
6. ___ you like music?
7. ___ he work here?
8. Did they ___ to school yesterday?

答案：

1. am
2. is
3. were
4. studies
5. like
6. Do
7. Does
8. go

## 八、📌 快速回顾

- `be` 动词现在时：`am / is / are`
- `be` 动词过去时：`was / were`
- 一般现在时第三人称单数：动词通常加 `s`
- 一般现在时疑问句：`Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形`
- 一般过去时疑问句：`Did + 主语 + 动词原形`
- `be` 动词疑问句：直接把 `be` 放到主语前面
